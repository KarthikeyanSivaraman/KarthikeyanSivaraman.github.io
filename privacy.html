Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What this covers

This policy covers "Faceless Channel Analysis", a set of personal scripts that Karthikeyan Sivaraman uses to manage his own YouTube channel, AI Data Simplified. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • Uploading videos and setting their details on the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API).
  • Reading view counts, watch time and subscriber figures for the owner's own channel (YouTube Analytics API, read-only).

It does not access any other person's account or data.

How data is stored and shared

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared.
  • Channel statistics are kept in the owner's private files for his own analysis.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
