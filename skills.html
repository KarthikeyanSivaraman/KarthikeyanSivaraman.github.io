Support Desk AI Assistant

Done
GenAIEmbeddings RetrievalGemini APIPrompt Injection DefenseStreamlitPydantic

A support-desk assistant that drafts cited replies for a person to approve (nothing is sent without approval). Uses Gemini embeddings only (gemini-embedding-001) after hybrid keyword + embeddings was tested and scored lower on this data.
