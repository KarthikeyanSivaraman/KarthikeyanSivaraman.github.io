Flagship A: IT/OT Predictive Maintenance Platform

Active Build • Data Pack Shipped

An end-to-end industrial equipment health system designed to prove senior data engineering, distributed deep learning, and agentic AI capabilities without invented enterprise data.

Core Design Principle

Recruiters skim; hiring managers go deep. Rather than twenty disjoint toy demos, Flagship A implements one cohesive, production-grade architecture in the industrial IoT domain. Every requirement in modern data/ML engineering job descriptions maps to a visible, benchmarked layer in this system.

Skills it shows: IT/OT, distributed training, LLM fine-tuning and serving, agentic AI, Azure ML, CI/CD, for data engineering, ML engineering and AI engineering roles.

End-to-End System Architecture

The platform spans real-time sensor streaming, lakehouse medallion storage, distributed training, low-latency inference, and human-in-the-loop maintenance agents.

Real Public Research Data

NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE)

C-MAPSS Turbofan Engine Degradation Simulation: Run-to-failure sensor data from simulated commercial turbofan engines under varying operating conditions and degradation regimes.

Provenance: Public NASA dataset manually downloaded by user. Not committed to version control; loaders read local files.

Locally Generated Synthetic Data

Seeded, Deterministic, Zero Network Calls

Fleet Identities & Procedures: Four synthetic sites (SITE-NORTH, SITE-SOUTH, SITE-EAST, SITE-WEST) and fictional NorthStar TF-200 equipment identities.

Maintenance Logs: 2,000 synthetic technician logs across 15 fault codes (seed 7). 1,600 train / 200 eval / 200 test.
Manuals Corpus: 12 Markdown files covering NorthStar TF-200 operating specifications and fault resolution steps.

Safety & Compliance Statement

Synthetic equipment, site names, technician notes, and operating manuals are explicitly illustrative evaluation artifacts. They do NOT represent real machinery or certified maintenance guidance. No proprietary employer or client data was used or referenced.
