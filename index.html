Support Desk AI

Live Eval Done
Gemini API Embeddings Retrieval Flash-Lite Human Approval Gate Injection Defense

A support-desk assistant that drafts cited replies for a person to approve (nothing is sent without approval). Uses Gemini embeddings only (gemini-embedding-001) after hybrid keyword + embeddings was tested and scored lower on this data. Drafts generated with Gemini Flash-Lite on the free tier.

Held-Out Test (40 tickets): Right article 30/30; required facts 88/94; unanswerable routed 9/10; answerable wrongly routed 1/30; injection marker absent 3/3. Decision requests routed to a person: 6/6 (dev set). Demo replays 9 example tickets.
